THE PROGRAMME

Course overview

This advanced course is designed to provide a deep dive into the critical components of machine learning workflows, focusing on feature engineering, model validation, hyperparameter tuning, and deployment. Through a combination of theoretical knowledge and hands-on projects, participants will learn how to refine and optimize machine learning models for real-world applications. The course culminates in an end-to-end project, giving participants the opportunity to apply their skills from data preprocessing to model monitoring in a practical scenario.
