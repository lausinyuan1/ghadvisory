THE PROGRAMME

Course overview

Designed for business leaders and sustainability advocates, this guide explores ESG principles with a focus on climate consciousness and sustainable practices. It offers insights into environmental, social, and governance strategies, combining research, case studies, and practical applications to help organizations integrate responsible practices into their core operations for long-term success.

WHAT’S COVERED

Training content

  • Definition of climate change and the climate change- related risks

  • The urgency of addressing climate change challenges.

  • Policy Advocacy and Global Initiatives Paris on Agreement, United National Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs), Kigali

  • Agreement and National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR)

  • Group discussion on Support Green Smart Manufacturing and Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol

  • Mitigation and Adaptation Strategies to climate change.

  • Identify ways to reduce carbon footprint and measurable Impact of reducing GHG emissions
