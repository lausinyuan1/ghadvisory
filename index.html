06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before we begin.

Ask us anything →
How long does it take to get started?+

Typically 30–60 days from signing to full go-live. We begin with an HR assessment, configure your payroll system, migrate your data and run a test payroll before launch.

What information do we need to provide?+

We guide you through an onboarding checklist: employee roster, salary details, statutory contribution history, and attendance and leave records. You provide the data; we handle the setup.

What happens if our headcount changes?+

Payroll is adjusted when staff join or leave. Pricing is updated the following month based on your new headcount, with no penalties or renegotiation required.

Do you handle foreign workers as well?+

Yes. We manage foreign worker payroll, applicable statutory contributions and levy tracking, with passport and permit expiry monitoring. Immigration permit applications are referred to a licensed partner.

Is there a lock-in period?+

The standard agreement has a 12-month initial term, renewable annually. After the initial term, either party may terminate with 30 days’ written notice.

What if we already have some HR processes?+

We tailor the scope to what you need, whether that is the full package or just payroll and statutory support. We can complement your existing setup.
