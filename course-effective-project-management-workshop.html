THE PROGRAMME

Course overview

This training introduces participants to the fundamentals of project management. It covers key concepts such as project planning, execution, monitoring, and closure. Attendees will learn practical skills for managing resources, timelines, and stakeholder expectations effectively, ensuring successful project outcomes. Through interactive sessions, they will apply these principles in real-world scenarios.
