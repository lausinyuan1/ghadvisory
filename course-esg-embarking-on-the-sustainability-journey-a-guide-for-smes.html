THE PROGRAMME

Course overview

Designed for small and medium enterprises, this guide empowers SMEs to embark on their sustainability journey. It offers insights into sustainable practices, effective resource management, and environmental responsibility. Combining industry research, practical examples, and actionable strategies, the guide enables businesses to integrate sustainability into core operations and drive long-term success.

WHAT’S COVERED

Training content

  • Introduction to Sustainability & ESG

  • Identify the key concepts of sustainability, including environmental,

  • social, and governance (ESG) factors.

  • Identify Key Sustainability & ESG

  • Recognize the importance of sustainability for businesses.

  • Data Collection and Management, Report Generation

  • Identify practical steps to incorporate sustainability practices into daily

  • life and business operations

  • Develop a Sustainability Roadmap and action plan to embark on business sustainability journey.
