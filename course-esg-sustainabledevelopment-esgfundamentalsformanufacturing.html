THE PROGRAMME

Course overview

Designed for manufacturing leaders and sustainability advocates, this guide delves into ESG fundamentals and sustainable development practices tailored for the manufacturing sector. It provides insights into integrating responsible practices into production and operations, combining industry research, practical examples, and actionable strategies for long-term success.

WHAT’S COVERED

Training content

  • Looking Back: MDG, SDG, CSR and ESG and interconnectivity

  • Discuss ESG Landscape and issues

  • Frameworks, Standards and Ratings (GRI, ISSB, IFRS etc)

  • Awareness on Possible Greenwashing Claims and Greenhouse Gas Emissions Boundaries

  • Case Study on Sustainability in Manufacturing industry (Energy Management, Waste Management, Sustainability Sourcing, DEI and Fair Labor Practice etc)

  • Breakouts and Workshops on Understanding an organization’s sustainability direction, strategy and Building actionable ESG practices
